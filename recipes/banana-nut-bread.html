Banana Nut Bread

Home

Ingredients

  • 3 ripe bananas
  • 1/3 cup melted butter
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • pinch of salt
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 cup chopped nuts (optional)

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C) and grease a 4x8-inch loaf pan.
  2. In a large bowl, mash the ripe bananas with a fork until smooth.
  3. Mix in the melted butter, sugar, beaten egg, baking soda, salt, and vanilla extract.
  4. Add the all-purpose flour and chopped nuts (if using) and mix until just combined.
  5. Pour the batter into the prepared loaf pan and smooth the top with a spatula.
  6. Bake for 50-60 minutes or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
  7. Let the bread cool in the pan for 10 minutes, then transfer to a wire rack to cool completely before slicing.
