Homemade Lemonade

Home

Ingredients

serving size: 2~

  • 5 yellow lemons
  • ½ cup granulaed sugar
  • 4 cups of cold water
  • to liking: ice cubes

Assembly

  1. Dissolve Sugar to prevent grittiness in a small amount of hot water before mixing with cold water and lemon juice
  2. Squeeze lemons to gather juice (approx. 1 cup). Set aside.
  3. In a medium sized pitcher, combine; water, lemon juice and sugar. Mix all ingredients well until all is well combined.
  4. Serve in an individual glass cup. Fill with ice to liking and enjoy!
  5. Optional: Garnish with lemon slice and mint
